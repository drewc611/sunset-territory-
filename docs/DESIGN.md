# Design direction
Painterly frontier, not photoreal and not cartoon. Warm dusty light, long shadows, faceted distant hills, detailed near ground.
HUD: serif on dark leather strips at the screen edges, nothing in the center except the discovery stamp. Fonts: Rye for display, Vollkorn for body. Palette: ink #1b140d, paper #d8c7a1, leather #6b4a2b, brass #c9a24d.
Banned: centered glass cards, purple gradients, sans serif HUD, health bars that look like a mobile RPG.
